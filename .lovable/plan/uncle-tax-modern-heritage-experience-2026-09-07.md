# Uncle Tax Modern Heritage Experience

## Goal
Turn the whole PAK TAX DIGITAL INVOICE site into a distinctly Pakistani, character-led service journey. Uncle Tax will feel like the trusted expert who handles invoicing, FBR digital invoicing and tax filing, while the product remains credible and professional.

## Visual direction
- Use the selected **Modern Heritage Guide** composition with the locked **Pakistan Ledger** palette: deep green, paper ivory, official blue and restrained brass.
- Change typography to **Archivo Black** for bold signboard-style headings and **Hind** for clear, localized body copy.
- Replace the current pale SaaS/glass-heavy presentation with warm paper surfaces, ledger rules, filing tabs, receipt edges and restrained stamp details.
- Keep light and dark modes, with the dark version feeling like a deep green records office rather than a blue tech dashboard.

## Uncle Tax as the guide
- Use the uploaded uncle illustration as the visual reference and primary character asset.
- Make Uncle Tax a strong first-screen signal beside the main offer, with a concise Urdu-English welcome and direct portal actions.
- Build a guided zigzag route through the page. Uncle Tax reappears in purposeful poses or cropped callouts to explain setup, invoicing, FBR posting, stock records and IRIS filing.
- Add contextual speech notes such as practical reminders and “next step” prompts, avoiding childish dialogue or excessive mascot repetition.
- Use Uncle Tax in the closing contact area as the handoff to Mr Jawad Haider by phone or WhatsApp.

## Page structure and interactions
1. **Header:** retain the PAK TAX brand, accurate FBR integration reference, theme switch, Client Portal and Digital Invoicing Portal access.
2. **Opening scene:** Uncle Tax, localized headline, the three portal paths, authentic FBR Digital Invoicing and IRIS marks, plus a compact live invoice status visual.
3. **Choose your kaam:** three clearly separated portal choices presented as desk files or indexed ledger tabs.
4. **Guided journey:** a connected zigzag path from registration and product setup to invoice posting and IRIS return filing, with each stage activating as it enters view.
5. **Service tour:** retain real Pakistani business imagery, but frame each capability as Uncle Tax solving a recognizable local business problem.
6. **Industries:** use shop, warehouse, restaurant and accountant scenes with localized hover details.
7. **Trust and answers:** retain testimonials and FAQs, restyled as stamped notes and an advice register.
8. **Contact close:** prominent Call and WhatsApp actions, exact contact details, all three portals and the origami99.com footer credit.

## Motion and engagement
- Give Uncle Tax subtle scroll-linked movement, pointing cues and speech-note entrances.
- Animate a receipt trail through the journey, with stamps appearing for “FBR Posted”, “ATL Checked” and “IRIS Filed”.
- Add responsive hover states to ledger tabs, service steps, portal choices and imagery.
- Keep existing floating Call and WhatsApp conversion controls and the mobile action bar.
- Respect reduced-motion settings and keep all interactions usable by touch and keyboard.

## Brand and trust rules
- Keep the new minimal receipt-fold “P” identity for PAK TAX DIGITAL INVOICE and integrate it into the heritage system rather than replacing it with the mascot.
- Preserve the corrected FBR, Digital Invoicing and IRIS marks only where their related service is described, with wording that communicates integration rather than government ownership.
- Do not use an Indian rupee symbol. Use PKR or Rs where currency context is needed.
- Keep all three existing portal URLs, Mr Jawad Haider’s contact details, no pricing and no em dashes.

## Technical details
- Add the uploaded Uncle Tax artwork through the project asset flow and create reusable character-led guide components.
- Update global semantic tokens, font loading, light/dark themes, paper/ledger/stamp treatments and reduced-motion animations.
- Recompose the home page around the selected direction while reusing the existing portal logic, contact controls, verified marks and real business images.
- Update the home page metadata to reflect the Uncle Tax guided FBR invoicing and filing service.
- Verify desktop and mobile layouts, both colour modes, all portal/contact links, scroll interactions, legibility and non-overlap.
