# Correct FBR marks, IRIS mark, and a properly designed brand logo

## 1. What the real marks look like

I checked the official sources you sent. Three separate marks matter:

- **FBR (Federal Board of Revenue)**: the letters "FBR" in deep blue with "PAKISTAN" underneath, a green swoosh sweeping over the top and a small gold star. Often locked up with the line "Federal Board of Revenue / Revenue Division - Government of Pakistan".
- **FBR Digital Invoicing**: a blue rounded "D" built from a thick stroke, with a green stepped block inside the lower left, next to the grey words "DIGITAL INVOICING".
- **IRIS**: the IRIS wordmark used on iris.fbr.gov.pk for the filing portal.

The green-and-gold crest currently on the site is nothing like any of these, so it goes away completely.

## 2. Replacing what is on the site now

- Delete the invented crest everywhere it appears: header, hero badge row, floating invoice card, how it works, contact strip, footer.
- Put in its place accurate recreations, drawn as crisp vector art so they stay sharp at any size and in both dark and light mode:
  - **FBR mark** in the header strip, the hero trust row, the closing compliance line and the footer.
  - **Digital Invoicing mark** on the hero invoice card and on the FBR posting step in "how it works", because that is the exact scheme our invoicing portal talks to.
  - **IRIS mark** on the tax filing step and beside the filing feature, since returns go through IRIS.
- Every placement is a "works with" badge, worded plainly as "Integrated with FBR Digital Invoicing" or "Filing through IRIS", never implying we are FBR. These are government marks, so if FBR ever objects we swap them for text badges in one edit.

## 3. New logo for PAK TAX DIGITAL INVOICE

No rupee glyph at all, since the wrong Indian one kept creeping in. The concept instead:

**A receipt that folds into the letter P.**

A single thick stroke, deep FBR blue, drawn as a slim receipt slip whose top curls over into the bowl of a "P". The bottom edge of the slip is torn in the small zigzag every POS receipt has. Inside the bowl sits one short green tick, the same green as the FBR swoosh, reading as "verified and posted". Two thin grey lines suggest the invoice rows. Nothing else.

Why it works: it is one letter, so it is readable at browser-tab size; the torn edge instantly says receipt and point of sale; the tick says tax cleared; the blue and green tie us visually to FBR and to the Digital Invoicing mark without copying either.

The mark is drawn as vector art, not a generated picture, so edges are perfectly clean. The browser icon is the same P alone. In the header it sits next to the words "PAK TAX" in bold with "DIGITAL INVOICE" smaller underneath.
